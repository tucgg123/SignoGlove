Calibrate the sensor

See what the phone measures in real time and choose hand positions that feel comfortable.

  1. Place the phone on your hand the way you’ll use it and tap “Start sensor”.
  2. For each phrase, put your hand in a comfortable position, hold still and tap “Save position”.
  3. Check below that each position says the right phrase, then tap “Use this calibration”.
